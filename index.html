PUBLICATION NO. 01

~ COOKING IN MINUTES ~

In this edition, we are inspired by the spirit shown in the foreword of the found notebook, where the author wholeheartedly gave their time and effort, in the form of handwritten cooking knowledge, to their dear one.
We start our journey with the questions: “Can we make something together?” and “How do we make everyone who is part of it feel seen, and their labour acknowledged?”

With a gathering and an apéro packaged together with the publication, creating a shared experience between the participants, we are given the chance to ponder how these questions can be answered and what the outcome could be...

OUR PURPOSE

To not let individuals feel overshadowed by centralized voices, we decided to create a(nother) platform for u to share what’s on your mind(perspective), at the same time, within a framework to create connections between people. Most important of all, for all to have fun and enjoyable art experiences!!
